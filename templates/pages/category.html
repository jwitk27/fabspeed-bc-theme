How Carbon Fiber Parts Are Made

  • Preparation: Polyacrylonitrile (PAN) fibers are spun, woven into sheets, and stabilized through heating.
  • Resin Impregnation: The fibers are impregnated with resin to strengthen and bond the material.
  • Molding and Curing: The resin-impregnated sheets are laid into molds and heated to cure the resin. The final curing occurs in a high-pressure oven called an autoclave.
  • Finishing: The part is trimmed, polished, and inspected before installation or shipping.
