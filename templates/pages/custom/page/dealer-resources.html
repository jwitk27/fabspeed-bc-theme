CALIFORNIA WHOLESALE DEALERS:

Streetcars, convertibles, cruisers, show cars, and daily drivers are ineligible for any restricted/race products, regardless of pre-existing gear, replacements, or customer desire/demand.

Vehicles registered for street use do not qualify for any race/restricted-sale products.

Track-Only Vehicle Qualification Requirements:

Rollbar, 6-point racing belt/harness, race brake pads, Road Racing slick special tires, trailered and never driven on the street, etc.

Purchasing a restricted/race product confirms your acknowledgement that the car will no longer be EPA/CARB compliant, and will not pass Federal or Local emissions inspections and may be subject to significant fines.

We look forward to supporting you with CARB-compliant products soon! Please call us at (215) 646-4945 or email wholesale@fabspeed.com with any questions.

FABSPEED MOTORSPORT LOGO DISCLAIMER: Fabspeed Authorized Dealers are permitted to use the Fabspeed Motorsport logo(s) and branding for promotional purposes.  We ask that you utilize the logo(s) in good taste, and do not misrepresent the Fabspeed brand.  All logos must be used in their original proportions and may not be distorted in any way.  If you have any questions regarding proper usage, or if you require a specific format, please contact our Marketing Department.

 

PRICING AND MAP POLICY

Fabspeed Motorsport requires that all authorized dealers adhere to our Minimum Advertised Pricing (MAP) Policy.  This policy states that no dealer may advertise or offer any Fabspeed Motorsport product for a price lower than the specified retail price.  You can find the current retail pricing by visiting www.fabspeed.com or by downloading and referencing the following printable price guide.
